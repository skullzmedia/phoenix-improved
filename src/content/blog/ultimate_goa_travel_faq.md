# 1. When is the best time to visit Goa? 🗓️
Peak season (Nov–Feb) brings pleasant 20–30 °C weather—perfect for beach days, parties, and festivals like the Goa Carnival and Sunburn. March–May offers fewer crowds and lower [hotel rates](https://www.tripadvisor.in/SmartDeals-g297604-Goa-Hotel-Deals.html#SPLITVIEWMAP) despite higher temps. Monsoon (Jun–Sep) is ideal for waterfall treks and lush scenery, though water sports are generally halted.

# 3. How do I reach Goa from Delhi or Mumbai? ✈️🚆🚗
- Air: Fly into Dabolim (GOI) or MOPA (GOX).
- Train: Take the scenic Konkan Railway for a coastal journey.
- Road: Drive along NH66 from Mumbai—about 10 hours, with scenic coastal views.

# 4. Can I rent and ride a scooter in Goa? 🛵
Yes! Rental shops near Calangute, Baga, Candolim and Panjim charge ₹300–500/day. A valid license (and IDP for foreigners) is mandatory, plus helmets. Rentals usually require ID and a deposit.

# 5. Why aren’t Ola or Uber available in Goa?
Ride-hailing services are restricted by local taxi unions. Instead, use [GoaMiles](https://www.goamiles.com/) (official taxi app), rent scooters, or hail local taxis—often more reliable and familiar with the area.

# 6. Which beaches are best for water sports? 🌊
Go-to beaches for jet skiing, parasailing, banana boat rides, and bumper rides include: Baga, Calangute, Candolim, Anjuna, Vagator, and Morjim. These hotspots are vibrant, safe, and lifeguard-patrolled.

# 7. Are there quieter, [hidden beaches](https://www.goa.gov.in/what_to_see/beaches/) in Goa?
Yes:
- Ashwem & Mandrem offer peace and yoga retreats.
- Arambol attracts free-spirited travelers with chilled-out vibes.
- Butterfly Beach is accessible only by boat, offering secluded beauty.

# 8. Is swimming safe at Goa’s beaches?
Generally safe when swimming in lifeguard-patrolled zones. Always heed red flags—especially during monsoon—or ask around before taking a dip.
