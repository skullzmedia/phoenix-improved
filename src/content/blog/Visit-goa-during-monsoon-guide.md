# 🌧️ What’s Monsoon in Goa Like?

Goa’s monsoon season runs from June to September, with the heaviest rain usually in July. Expect:
- Lush green landscapes everywhere
- Cooler temperatures (25–30°C)
- Fewer crowds and more peace
- Off-season hotel and flight deals

While water sports and some beach shacks close during this time, many top attractions, casinos, and nightlife spots remain open—especially in North Goa.

For official weather details and updates, check: https://mausam.imd.gov.in
