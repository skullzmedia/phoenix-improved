## Offshore vs Onshore Casinos in Goa: What’s the Difference?

When you think about casinos in Goa, the image that probably comes to mind is glamorous floating vessels anchored on the Mandovi River. But that’s only half the story. Goa’s casino scene is actually split into two distinct categories: offshore (floating) casinos and onshore (land-based) casinos. While both offer exciting gaming experiences, they operate under different rules, feature different types of games, and provide completely different atmospheres.
As of 2025, Goa hosts 13 officially licensed casino, 6 offshore vessels and 7 onshore establishments. Understanding the differences between these two types can help you choose the perfect gaming experience for your next Goa visit.
The Legal Framework Behind Goa’s Dual Casino System
Goa stands out as one of only few Indian states where casinos are legally permitted.
 
The law makes a clear distinction between the two types of casinos. Offshore casinos are permitted to operate on vessels anchored in designated areas of the Mandovi River in Panaji, while onshore casinos can only function within five-star hotels and luxury resorts. This legal separation isn’t just administrative, it fundamentally shapes what each type of casino can offer.
In August 2025, the Goa government introduced new draft rules giving the gaming commissioner enhanced powers to monitor casino operations, issue entry tickets, and track visitor records . Additionally, all offshore casino licenses were extended until March 31, 2027, providing stability to the industry while regulators work on long-term policies.

## Offshore Casinos: The Floating Entertainment Hubs
Offshore casinos are perhaps Goa’s most iconic gaming destinations. These massive vessels are permanently anchored at specific mooring points along the Mandovi River in Panaji, creating what locals call a “floating casino corridor”.

What Makes Them Special:
The biggest advantage of offshore casinos is that they’re legally permitted to host live table games. This means you can enjoy authentic casino experiences with real dealers at games like poker, blackjack, roulette, and baccarat. The atmosphere on these floating venues tends to be more glamorous and grand, with many casinos featuring multiple decks, VIP gaming zones, rooftop sundecks, and full-scale entertainment options.
Major offshore players include Deltin Royale, Big Daddy, Casino Pride, Casino Pride 2, and Deltin JAQK . These vessels operate 24/7 and are accessible via private shuttle services from various pickup points in Panaji and North Goa.
The Experience:
Visiting an offshore casino feels like boarding a luxury cruise. You typically take a short ferry ride from the mainland, which adds to the sense of occasion. Once aboard, you’re greeted with multiple gaming floors, live entertainment shows, gourmet buffets, premium bars, and often live music or DJ performances. Entry packages usually range from ₹2,000 to ₹6,000 and include meals, unlimited beverages, gaming chips, and entertainment.
The Downsides:
The main disadvantage is accessibility. You need to factor in travel time to the jetty and ferry schedules. Additionally, offshore casinos can feel crowded during peak tourist season, especially on weekends and holidays. There have also been ongoing controversies about their environmental impact on the Mandovi River, with the government periodically discussing plans to relocate them to land-based entertainment zones.
